**Privacy Policy for Local Lens**

**Last Updated:** February 20th, 2026

**Data Collection and Usage**

Local Lens is a privacy-first extension designed to run entirely on your device.

- **Website Content:** The extension captures screenshots of the active tab only when you explicitly trigger the "crop" action. These images are processed locally within your browser using bundled PaddleOCR models that ship with the extension.
- **No Data Transmission:** Local Lens does not collect, store, or transmit your screenshots, extracted text, or browsing history to any external servers. All processing happens in your browser's memory and local storage.

**3. Contact**

If you have questions about this policy, please create an issue on `https://github.com/minhkhoango/local-lens`
