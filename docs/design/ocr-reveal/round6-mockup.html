Local Lens · result UI · design study 06 — Capsule

The drag is on screen now, and ready is clean

Three changes. The drag and release is animated — the rectangle is pulled out of its corner, the scrim opens with it, a live size chip follows the cursor, and mouse-up gives one ring before reading starts. Scrub to Drag, or just watch.

The region is never dimmed or blurred as a whole now, at any stage. The only thing that ever appears inside it is a mark on a run that was read — arriving in a wave while the model works, settling into a slight overlay at ready. That is a confirmation, not a cover. Confirm switches how heavy it is, including one that fades to nothing; Blur switches how much softening rides along during the read, including none at all.

And the capsule says Fast / Structured again, with the real model names moved into the menu where there is room for them. That collapsed two of last round's versions into one, so the four below are now different layouts, not different label widths.

What the four versions actually differ on now

VersionReading orderThe idea being tested
V1 · Namedstate → name → Copy → closeThe conventional one. You always know which model produced this.
V2 · Capsule is the buttonstate → Copy → name → closeAt ready the pill itself goes primary, so the thing you came to do is the whole object rather than a chip inside it.
V3 · One slotstate or name → Copy → closeNever carries both. Narrowest exactly while something is happening.
V4 · Wordlessstate → ▾ → ⧉ → ✕No text at rest at all. Smallest possible footprint; costs you the name.
Open — pick freely, and reject the framing too if it is wrong:
  • Which layout. They can also be mixed: V2's leading Copy with V3's one slot, for instance.
  • Confirmation weight. Wash / Underline / Sweep. Sweep leaves the region completely clean after a second — that may be better or may feel like nothing happened.
  • Blur while reading. Feather / Light / Soft, or drop the reading blur entirely and let the marks alone carry it.
  • Whether the capsule should dim during the drag at all, or stay solid.
  • Whether the size chip that follows the cursor is worth its noise.
  • Whether Copy stays filled once it is the only loud thing on screen.
